# Underfoot — store page

**Tagline:** One ant. One backyard. One day, the whole house.

## Short description
Grow a single ant burrow into a backyard empire. Dig living nests that breathe, flood and rot; lead foragers with scent instead of orders; survive spiders, birds, rival ants and the lawnmower. Then invade the house, if you dare.

## About the game
You wake up as a lone queen in an abandoned suburban backyard. At ant scale, the sprinkler is a flood, the barbecue is a volcano, the garden gnome is a mountain and the cat is a god.

- **Two worlds at once.** Switch between the side-on nest and the top-down lawn. Both keep simulating.
- **Ants follow pheromones, not orders.** Paint, strengthen and erase scent trails, then watch the colony optimise its own logistics.
- **Living architecture.** Air goes stale, rain seeps down, mould spreads. Dig vents, sumps and pillars or watch your chambers flood and cave in.
- **Raise the colony you need.** Feed larvae protein for soldiers or sugar for scouts. Nurses carry brood to the warmest chamber.
- **Seasons.** A spring boom, deadly summer heat, autumn stockpiling and a winter siege underground.
- **Evolve.** Nuptial flights earn trait points across five branches: trap-jaws, formic spray, aphid ranching, fungus farming, living bridges, hive mind and more.
- **The house.** Raid the kitchen without being seen. Every sighting raises the alarm, from wiped counters to bait traps, sprays and finally the Exterminator.
- **73-species Field Guide.** Meet (and sometimes eat) the neighbours. Befriend a rhinoceros beetle and ride it into battle.
- **Campaign, Sandbox and the seeded Endless Yard.**
